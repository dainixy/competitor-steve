Standout & Actions for Steve Session complete · 2026-05-20

SecretDesires is a capable AI brain wrapped in an unstable product. The chat wit, auto-memory transparency, and voice pacing are genuinely competitive — possibly the best underlying model of all competitors tested. But every interaction is undermined by a broken feedback layer: no progress indicators, 500 errors on profile fields, image upload failures, character creation bugs, a site that feels slow and jank throughout. They're building fast without QA. Their "YouTube of AI companions" vision is ambitious; the current execution doesn't support it. 338K visits/month despite this suggests real demand — they're growing on feature breadth, not polish.

Steal immediately for DRT.FM

  • Transparent auto-memory as character's own voice. SD's auto-memory shows what the character remembers in first-person narrative ("I remember Dainis seemed distracted…"). Far more immersive than a data list. Steve should build memory display this way.
  • Two-step escalating cancel bribe. Offer 1: 2× hearts, same price. Offer 2 (if declined): 2.5× hearts, "only available right now." Clear conversion pattern — implement before launch.
  • Visual outfit + action pickers with photo thumbnails. Bikini/Lace/Thongs/etc. shown as example images, not text labels. Users browse, not type. Same approach as OurDream — confirmed good by both sessions.
  • Voice pacing. SD speaks at natural conversational speed. Every other competitor is too slow and kills immersion. DRT.FM voice defaults must match this cadence.
  • Prompt copy button in image details. Exposes the exact prompt used for each generated image with a copy button. Power-user feature that drives iteration and reduces frustration.

DRT.FM already does better

  • Platform stability. SD has 500 errors, empty-page refreshes, image upload failures, and a broken character creation final step. If DRT.FM runs clean, that alone is a competitive advantage.
  • Generation feedback. SD has zero progress indicators on video gen, character creation, or image upload. Users genuinely don't know if anything is happening. Any loading state is better than none.
  • Homepage energy. SD's homepage is static images, no motion, no social proof, hidden creator usernames. Feels like a catalogue. If DRT.FM has animated previews and visible creator identity, it wins first impression.
  • Onboarding. SD puts a dense guide in the top nav and expects users to read it. Candy AI's in-chat assistant is better. Contextual help beats documentation.

Avoid / counter-position

  • Per-partner settings required to unlock media — with no explanation. Voice and images are silenced until you activate a hidden per-partner toggle. Users assume it's broken. Always explain what needs to be enabled and why.
  • Image engine visible on video tab without explanation. Confuses users who don't know the image engine seeds the animation. Either hide it or add one line of context.
  • Cancel flow communication creates anxiety before reassuring. 4 steps of friction before the confirmation screen. The confirmation itself is warm and clear ("your space stays open until June 20") but users are anxious throughout. Consider front-loading the reassurance: tell users upfront they keep access, then offer the bribes.
  • Wise / international card declines. SD blocked Wise on a Malta-entity routing issue. Confirm Stripe accepts Wise on DRT.FM — the AI companion demographic skews digital-native and Wise is common.
  • Video gallery with no filter and a tiny star icon for videos. Users can't tell photos from videos at a glance. Build a Photos / Videos tab filter from day one.
  • No preset action prompts for video. "Undress" gets ~50% compliance. Guide users with tappable action suggestions — casual users shouldn't need prompt engineering skills to get a result.

Cancel flow — two-step escalating retention bribes.
Bribe 1: "Stay to get 1 Month Bonus Hearts — we'll double your monthly hearts on next renewal." (300 → 600 hearts). Same price, cancel anytime. Bribe 2 (if you decline bribe 1): "Stay to get 2.5x Bonus Hearts — we'll give you 2.5x your monthly hearts on next renewal." (300 → 750 hearts). "This offer is only available right now." Also shown: "Here's what you'll lose" loss-aversion list (unlimited messaging · image gen · voice · extra hearts · hearts balance access). Exit survey with 5 canned reasons + freetext. CTA is "I don't want to cancel" (prominent pink) vs "I want to cancel" (plain text, low contrast).

Two-step escalating bribe is well-executed retention mechanic. DRT.FM should consider this pattern — offer grows if first offer is declined.

Full cancel flow — actually 4 steps, and access is fully preserved. The complete cancel sequence:
  1. Goodbye screen + exit survey (exit reason checkboxes + freetext)
  2. Bribe 1: 2× hearts on next renewal
  3. Bribe 2: 2.5× hearts (if bribe 1 declined)
  4. "Downgrade instead?" modal — Switch to Ultra / Switch to Pro / Cancel anyway
Cancellation confirmation screen: "Your private space stays open until June 20, 2026 — you can still use your hearts while your subscription is still active — partners and chats remain safe — 2.5x bonus still applies if you reactivate." Billing page confirms: "you will be able to use the service until the end of the billing cycle." Access IS preserved as promised.

Earlier confusion: the communication felt ambiguous in the moment, but access and hearts both continue until June 20. The copy is reassuring once you see the confirmation screen — but anxiety before that point is high. The downgrade offer (step 4) is a third retention layer not seen on other competitors.
