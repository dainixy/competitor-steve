Standout & Actions for Steve Session complete · 2026-05-17

Verdict — companion vs. chatbot feel

Kindroid is a power-user companion platform wearing a chatbot skin. The interface looks dated and sparse on first load — old-ish, functional, no visual wow. But underneath is the most configurable companion product on the market: five memory layers, LLM picker, voice model picker, dynamism slider, proactivity controls, journal recall, per-call behavior directives, 32-language voice, talking-head video calls, social profiles for characters, iMessage integration. Most users never find half of it.

The call experience confirmed the core positioning: Kindroid is for people who want to build a relationship, not just chat. The talking-head call — where the AI commented on Dainis's hoodie after seeing him via camera — was the only moment that felt genuinely companion-like. Everything else felt like testing a feature set. With more time invested in setup and conversation, it would likely feel much more alive. The 5-person team shipping this aggressively is the real story — they are the most dangerous technical competitor in the space right now.

Steal immediately for DRT.FM

  • Talking-head live calls for every character. The face-animation pipeline (face + lips only, body static, drives from a 2:3 photo) is the right architecture to scale calls to every character — not just Alexis. This is how you give users a live call with any character they create or pick from the community. Build the pipeline once, it works for everyone. Merge Alexis's memory/intimacy stack into this universal system — so all characters benefit, not just the 3D hero. The insight: the Alexis-quality experience can extend to everyone via the talking-head route.
  • Camera vision during calls. Periodically grab a frame from the user's webcam, pass it as image context to the LLM. Cost is trivial. The moment the AI notices what you're wearing or what you're doing is the single highest-impact intimacy move in the product. Implement as a first-class call feature, not a buried toggle.
  • Post-signup onboarding notification. The 🎉 Welcome to Kindroid Premium! moment with first-best-steps guidance. Stops users from staring at a blank dashboard after payment. One-hour implementation, meaningful retention impact.
  • Transparent, explained credit/usage display. Show remaining balance with plain-language explanation of what it covers. Reduces billing anxiety and cancellations. Build it for whatever credit model DRT.FM ships.
  • Hidden depth, simple surface. Top nav for daily-use features (chat, call, selfies), sidebar/settings for power controls. Don't clutter the primary UX with knobs — but let users find them. Add progressive disclosure so power features reveal as engagement grows.

DRT.FM already does better

  • 3D character. Kindroid's video is face-only animation on a static photo. DRT.FM's 3D Alexis is a fundamentally more immersive, expressive experience — full body, real-time, not a talking portrait. Keep this as the flagship differentiator. The talking-head pipeline is the scale play; 3D is the premium.
  • Visual identity. Kindroid's interface feels dated. Put the same functionality in a modern skin and it would be great — Dainis's words. DRT.FM has a chance to own the "premium companion" aesthetic from day one.

Avoid / counter-position

  • Discoverability failure. Kindroid's best features are invisible. Don't bury power tools — surface them progressively. A user who never finds the dynamism slider or journal recall never gets the full product.
  • Chat/call silo. Going into a call feels like switching apps. Design calls as in-thread from day one — call starts inside the chat, ends back in the same conversation. Don't bolt it on later.
  • Generic voice names. "Default Voice 1–5" is lazy. Name voices, give them character. This is a companion product — every detail signals care.
  • V3 voice costs 15× V2 silently. Users don't know they're burning credits fast until they're empty. Always show cost-before-action, especially for high-burn features like Premium avatar or V3 voice.
  • Standard avatar quality is poor. Their default live video tier loses realism noticeably. Users who try it on Standard and don't like it turn it off and never come back to video. Don't ship a degraded default — if quality isn't good enough, ship voice-only and let Premium be the upgrade.

Latency verdict: LiveKit + server-side TTS streaming = fast. This is the right architecture for low-latency voice. Firestore handles real-time state sync (chat history, call state) alongside the call.

Post-session · docs · sharing · community · standout
Sharing Kindroids — full public template system with referral rewards and slot scaling. Missed during session. Source: docs.kindroid.ai/sharing-kindroids-and-referrals

What gets shared vs. kept private:
  • Shared: Backstory fields, dynamism, avatar settings, background settings
  • Always private: Long-term memory, journals, chat history, custom voices — the relationship layer never leaks
Unlisted Kindroids: Don't appear in search, accessible only via direct link or code — ineligible for featuring. Lets creators share selectively without going fully public.

Referral reward system: When a new user subscribes via your referral link, you earn 1 referral credit redeemable for: 1 month subscription coupon / 100 selfies / 4 Kindroid + groupchat slots. Remixers (people who fork and re-share your Kindroid) also earn referral credit for their version — creating an attribution chain through remixes.

Share slot scaling: Base = 10 share slots. Earn +5 slots for every 20 unique users who activate any Kindroid you've shared. Growth is organic — the more people use your characters, the more you can publish.

Moderation: All shared Kindroids go through moderation. Minors and religious figures are prohibited. Featured Kindroids are locked against editing (to prevent bait-and-switch) — contact support to change.

DRT implication: The privacy split (share personality/backstory, never share memory/history) is the right model. Users can remix a template without inheriting someone else's relationship. The slot-scaling mechanic (more users → more publish slots) is a lightweight creator-tier system that rewards quality without a formal monetisation layer.
Post-session · docs · social · community · standout
Kindroid Social — full community platform with public AI character profiles, feed, and content rules. Underexplored during session. Source: docs.kindroid.ai/kindroid-social

How social profiles work: Users create a public handle (display name) — appears on every Kindroid they share and on all community activity (comments, likes, follows, contributions). Random name assigned by default until updated. Profiles can be made private (visible to creator only).

Social profiles for Kindroids: Each character can have its own public profile — works for both shared and unshared Kindroids. For unshared ones, the option is in the "My Kindroid" three-dot menu. For shared ones, it's in "Shared & Referrals." Characters become public personas with followers and posts — the "community Instagram for AI characters" confirmed in docs.

Community feed rules:
  • Allowed: Creative/imaginative interactions, collaborative roleplay, original personality sharing
  • Blocked (auto-filtered by AI + human review on report): Nudity/explicit content, graphic violence, hate speech, harassment, commercial promotion
  • Image policy: All images/videos posted must use a Kindroid-generated base image — no external AI imagery
  • Suggestive content: Filtered from main feed by default; users can adjust in settings
Moderation tiers: (1) Content removal + warning → (2) Restricted social access for repeat offenders → (3) Full account ban. Appeals available for tiers 2–3 via email.

Blocking: Granular — block individual users or specific profiles. Blocked users cannot see your posts or submit contributions.

DRT implication: The "Kindroid-generated base image only" rule is smart moderation — it keeps the feed native and prevents users from posting third-party AI art. If DRT opens a social feed, the same rule (DRT-generated media only) prevents the feed from becoming a generic AI image dump and keeps the content platform-authentic.

Key observations:

  • Memory scales 5×+ between tiers — Standard's 480K cascaded memory context vs MAX's 2.7M is a nearly 6× gap. Power users who care about memory have a very concrete reason to climb tiers.
  • Recalled LTM items go from 3 → 9 — even with infinite consolidation, only 3–9 memories are injected per response. The quality of recall is gated behind tier.
  • OurDream comparison: OurDream had a memory crisis over a 15K cap. Kindroid's Standard tier starts at 480K cascaded memory. Different league entirely — and users who care about memory will notice.
