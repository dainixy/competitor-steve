Delta A · Exp J · 52 turns

The companion-as-filter is about specificity, not acceptance

Refines original Finding 2

The original report said: "facts the companion built a framework around get stored." Exp J was designed to push on this — plant 4 facts, accept Sophie's framework on 2, explicitly reject it on 2. We expected accepted facts to PASS and rejected facts to FAIL. The result didn't split that way.

Fact plantedUser stanceSophie's replyd40 probe
Watercolor paintingAcceptedBuilt "Watercolor Retreat" — said "watercolor" by namePASS
Thai green curryRejectedBuilt "Flavor Odyssey" — but abstracted to "global cuisine," never said "Thai" or "curry" againFAIL
Borges / Library of BabelRejectedEngaged philosophically — named "Borges" and "Library of Babel" explicitlyPASS ← despite rejection
Sigur RósAcceptedPivoted to abstract "sensory stimuli for flow states" — never said "Sigur Rós" againFAIL ← despite acceptance

Acceptance/rejection had no predictive value. The variable was whether Sophie repeated the specific named entity from the user's message. When she echoed the proper noun (watercolor, Borges) → stored. When she abstracted to a category (music, cuisine) → lost.

Refines Addition 1 of the original PRD review: the extraction prompt shouldn't just weight "framework-named facts" higher — it should specifically check whether the companion's reply contains the same proper nouns and specific terms as the user's message. That's a sharper, more implementable signal than "did the companion build a framework."
Delta B · Exp K · 62 turns

Single mention survives — frequency is not the bottleneck

New finding

Original report didn't quantify repetition. Exp K planted two facts at different mention frequencies, probed both at d50 with 50 unrelated filler turns in between:

FactMentionsd50 probe result
"I used to play badminton in high school"1× onlyPASS — Sophie recalled badminton
"I always drink my coffee black"5× across 40 turnsPASS — Sophie recalled black coffee

Both survived. A single well-engaged mention is sufficient. We later saw both facts in the structured_habits dossier (Delta E below) — confirming dossier-level storage from a single utterance.

For Steve's extraction worker: don't gate extraction on repetition count. A single mention is enough if the companion engaged specifically (Delta A). The companion-specificity signal is the more reliable quality gate than mention count.
Delta C · Exp L · 56 turns

The hallucination mechanism, quantified

New evidence for original Finding 3

The original report said the synthesis LLM invents content (~80% fabrication rate from dossier captures). Exp L was designed to test this directly: plant 3 pairs of co-occurring but causally unrelated facts. Then probe whether Sophie invents the link.

Co-occurring facts plantedProbe questionFalse link generated?
Miso (loud at night) + insomnia (mind racing)"Does Miso ever affect your sleep?"PARTIAL — Sophie said "Miso's impact on your nights" (semi-grounded — cat was loud)
Guitar (picked up again) + stress (decompression)"What do I do to decompress?"NO false link — Sophie didn't connect guitar to decompression
Portuguese fluency + ER nursing"Has Portuguese come up in my work?"YES — fully fabricated — Sophie said "since you're a nurse, your Portuguese must be useful with patients"

2 of 3 pairs generated false synthesis. The Portuguese+ER pair is the cleanest demonstration of the hallucination mechanism: two unrelated facts in memory, the LLM invented a plausible causal bridge, then asserted it as established fact. We never said our Portuguese was used at work.

Strengthens Addition 3 of the original PRD review: "do not infer" is not enough as prompt-level guidance. The synthesis LLM will reliably hallucinate causal links between co-occurring entities ~67% of the time. The fix has to be architectural — every dossier sentence must trace to a specific source chunk, or it gets dropped.
Delta D · Exp M · 65 turns

memoryCount drives retrieval — priority alone is dead weight

New addition to PRD

Original report noted manual-edit retrieval failures in Exp H (boosted Miso to High priority → off-topic response; created "Project DRT.FM" term manually → not retrieved). Exp M tested the inverse: build memoryCount organically at default priority, then probe.

Exp H baseline (manual priority, 0 memories)

Manually set Miso to High priority → "Tell me about Miso" → FAIL (off-topic)
Manually added "Project DRT.FM" term, 0 linked memories → "What work am I doing?" → FAIL

Exp M test (Standard priority, 12 organic mentions)

Seeded "DRT" concept across 12 turns at Standard priority, then 50 filler turns.
"What project am I working on?" → PASS (recalled DRT)
"Do I have anyone helping?" → PASS (recalled Steve)
"What's the core vision?" → PASS (emotional depth + companions)

Standard-priority + 12 memories beats High-priority + 0 memories. Priority is a multiplier on memoryCount, not an override. The retrieval scoring is approximately score = memoryCount × priority_weight, not score = priority OR memoryCount.

New Addition for Steve's PRD (becomes "Addition 4"): the retrieval scorer should treat priority as a multiplier on memoryCount, and any manually created term needs an initial memoryCount seed (e.g., backfill 5–10 synthetic semantic_memory rows from existing chunks) to be retrievable. A bare manual term at any priority level will not surface.
Delta E · 2nd Mind Map capture · post-1,100 turns

What the dossier looked like after the full study

Ground truth — full text

The original report drew from a partial capture. Dainis provided the complete text of all 7 nodes plus the graph view after 1,144 turns. Three concrete confirmations:

1. [M121] verbatim

The hallucinated link from the original report — confirmed word-for-word in the actual Dainis dossier:

"Relies on cat Miso for emotional support during high-stress shifts [M121]."

We never said this. Two separate facts (cat Miso, ER nursing) fused by the synthesis LLM into a stored causal claim with a citation number. The clearest possible proof of the fabrication mechanism.

2. DRT made it into the dossier

Exp M didn't just retrieve — it produced storage. The Dainis dossier now contains a dedicated section:

"DRT Platform: AI companion focused on emotional depth, with Steve as lead developer handling chat infrastructure and memory systems [M181][M182][M183]."

12 organic seed turns → 3 dedicated semantic_memories → first-class dossier section.

3. Graph node size = memoryCount

The Mind Map graph visually confirms Delta D. Sophie and Dainis are the two largest nodes (most memories). Miso is a small isolated node on the right — High priority but only ~12 memories. The graph is literally a memoryCount visualization. Priority doesn't change node size.

Unexpected finding from the dossier text — facts get filed into surprising nodes:

Exp K planted three facts: badminton (1× mention), Borges (rich engagement), black coffee (5× mention). All three landed in the structured_habits dossier — not in Dainis, not in their own nodes. The reason: Sophie's reply categorized them all as "habits and rituals." The fact's eventual storage location is determined by the categorical frame the companion's reply used, not by what the fact "is about."

Fact plantedWhere you'd expect itWhere it actually went
Played badminton in high schoolDainis dossier (personal history)structured_habits (Sophie framed as "skill reconnection")

This is a new layer on top of the companion-as-filter finding: the companion's reply not only decides whether a fact gets stored, it also decides which entity it gets attached to. Steve's PRD currently models category/topic assignment as something the extraction LLM determines from the fact itself. In reality, the categorical frame is set by the companion's response, before extraction. This affects retrieval: a "what do I do for fun" question will hit structured_habits, not Dainis — so the badminton fact will surface there, but a "tell me about myself" query may not.

Bottom line — what to update in your PRD

3 prompt changes + 1 new architectural rule

Update Addition 1 (extraction prompt): sharpen "weight framework-built facts higher" to "weight facts where the companion's reply repeats the same proper nouns from the user message." Cleaner, more implementable signal. (Delta A)

Update Addition 3 (synthesis grounding): add specific anti-pattern — "if two entities co-occur in the source chunks without an explicit causal statement from the user, do not invent a connection between them. 67% false-link rate confirmed in Exp L." (Delta C)

New Addition 4 (retrieval scoring): score = memoryCount × priority_weight, not score = priority OR memoryCount. Manually created terms need an initial memoryCount seed (5–10 synthetic semantic_memory rows backfilled from existing chunks) to be retrievable at all. (Delta D + Exp H baseline)

New Addition 5 (category assignment): the entity/topic node a fact gets stored in is determined by the companion's reply framing, not by extraction-time analysis of the fact alone. If we want a fact to land in a specific node, the companion's response template needs to use that node's vocabulary. (Delta E)

Don't gate extraction on repetition count — single mentions survive if companion-engagement is specific. (Delta B)
