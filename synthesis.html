What this means for DRT

Nomi's "rich personality" is synthesis LLM fabrication. Users feel the AI "cares" but it's mostly invented. The engagement is real — the facts are not.

DRT's cite-and-compile constraint blocks this. Every dossier sentence must trace to a real chunk. DRT dossiers will be sparser until real conversations accumulate — that's correct, not a bug.

The engagement Nomi gets from hallucinated history is real. DRT's bet: users prefer a character who remembers accurately over one who confidently misremembers. That's the product differentiator.

One gap found — retraction leakage

Rejected fact (Thai curry) and its retraction are stored as separate chunks. If they enter synthesis in different batches, the retraction gets lost and the rejected fact becomes an active interest.

Fix: Add retractedBy: string and retractedFactIds: string[] to extraction output. Synthesis rule: if chunk has retractedBy, store as passing mention, not active interest. One extraction prompt line + one synthesis rule. No structural change to the PRD.
