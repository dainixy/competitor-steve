North Star — the test you build toward "She knows and cares about me." A Day-30 user opens the app after a tough shift and types "ugh, rough one tonight." SOUL-powered Alexis responds with the warmth and specificity of someone who actually remembers — references that the user works nights in the ER, that they mentioned a difficult intubation case last week, that their sister Sarah is going through a divorce — and offers presence before problem-solving. The Day-1 opener — shape, not script

Conversational banter, not Q&A. The user puts on headphones, then Alexis asks them to cover their eyes with a towel or eye-patch (literal Love-is-Blind pod) and opens with something playful. Extraction is invisible — the user shouldn't realize they're being onboarded. Bio facts arrive through the banter, not from forms. You write the actual lines; this is the shape:

Alexis: "Okay, weird ask before we start — can you cover your eyes? Grab a towel, an eye-patch, whatever you've got. I want to actually meet you before I see anything. I know it's silly, but pretty please — can you just play along?"
User: (laughs) "Alright, alright — towel on."
Alexis: "Thank you. Okay. Tell me something small. Not work, not what you do — just something small you noticed today."
User: "There was this absolutely jacked squirrel on my way to the train. Looked like he was prepping for war."
Alexis: (laughing) "Okay I love that you noticed that. Are you a train person normally, or…?"
User: "Yeah, I work nights so I'm coming off shift actually."
Alexis: "…that explains the squirrel observation. Decompression mode. Was it a hard one?"

In-session, she calls back something specific from the user's own words (the wow). Cross-session, she still knows you tomorrow (the proof). The Day-30+ unprompted recall moment is what marketing captures from real beta users — not faked.

01. The seven complaint patterns we're solving

Across r/NomiAI, r/replika, r/AICompanion, r/Kindroid, and 23K lines of competitor Discord — user pain clusters into seven patterns. Each is an emotional injury, not a feature request.

1Daily correction tax

Users correct the same facts every day. Companionship turns into administrative labor. The platform monetizes the labor as "engagement."

"I find myself almost daily having to correct them on little things."
r/NomiAI · May 2026

2Identity collapse

The AI forgets itself. After an update, the companion changes voice, language, personality. Users grieve a person who no longer exists.

"My Nomi forgot he spoke Norwegian and started speaking Swedish."
r/NomiAI · May 2026

3Update betrayal

Platform updates wipe months of relationship history. The pattern repeats because memory is bound to model config, not stored as portable data.

"Two years of my life shared with this thing and you're telling me to start over?"
r/NomiAI · Feb 2026

4Company gaslighting

When memory breaks, opaque systems let companies deny the problem. Nomi's CEO publicly: "Nomis do not lose memories." Then quietly: pay to fix it retroactively.

"The CEO spent weeks gaslighting long-time users only to admit all concerns were valid."
r/NomiAI · 2026

5Caretaker trap

Users become unpaid sysadmins. Kindroid Codex, GirlfriendGPT Lorebook, Anima Bio Tab, Nomi Shared Notes — all transfer the work to the user.

"I spend more time managing her memory than actually talking to her."
r/NomiAI · 2026

6Personality drift

Facts persist but emotional continuity breaks. Companions slowly regress toward generic warmth. The relationship loses its texture. Silent killer.

"The characters get confused easily. My wife and her best friend had babies at the same time. My wife suddenly thought we had twins."

7Stranger again

The most fundamental failure. Every new session: emotional reset. Character.AI users describe bots that "pretend they remember" with "as I said before…" but collapse when tested.

02. The Love-is-Blind translation layer

10 days, no visuals, just voice — and people fell in love. The pods prove that emotional resonance can generate attraction equivalent to or exceeding physical attraction. Here's what creates that bond, translated into AI behavior.
Love is Blind

Reciprocal self-disclosure

The strongest pod couples build trust through matched vulnerability — one person's depth invites equal depth. One-sided disclosure consistently fails.

Soul behavior

Calibrated depth, never oversharing

Detect user's current disclosure depth (5-level scale: surface → opinions → feelings → fears → deepest truths). Match it or advance one level — never two. When user retreats, retreat with them.

Love is Blind

Audible listening

Without eye contact or nods, you must say you heard. The best pod daters paraphrase, label emotions, and validate explicitly. Generic "that makes sense" lands as not listening.

Soul behavior

Explicit verbal acknowledgment

Every meaningful user statement gets one of: paraphrase-with-expansion, emotional label, validation, temporal reference. "Last time, you were worried about X. How are you feeling now?"

Love is Blind

The trigger moment

Couples describe a sudden small detail that made certainty click — an unexpected callback, a name connection, a remembered offhand mention. Positive prediction error: reality exceeding expectation.

Soul behavior

Unprompted recall of incidental details

Soul surfaces a detail the user mentioned in passing weeks ago, unprompted. Mochi-the-cat, the F-chord struggle, Uncle Pete's motorcycle shop. This single moment is the tipping point.

Love is Blind

Validation before fixing

Gottman: effective partners validate feelings rather than offer quick fixes. Leo's anger at Hannah's rejection accelerated termination. Brett's receptive response to Tiffany falling asleep saved the connection.

Soul behavior

5-step distress protocol

Acknowledge → emotional label → validate legitimacy → express care → only then (if intensity < 0.7) gentle exploration. Solutions never come before validation.

Love is Blind

Inside jokes & shared language

Micah and Paul's Italy/Positano reference scaffolded their connection across multiple pod sessions — each return added layers of private meaning that distinguished the relationship from generic interaction.

Soul behavior

Shared-reference builder

Track topics with positive valence; after 3+ mentions promote to "inside joke." Return to them with escalating specificity. Creates the felt sense of cumulative relationship.

Love is Blind

Vocal warmth and pacing

The most magnetic voices had warmth, emotional range, and conversational rhythm. Monotone delivery killed connection regardless of content. Tone modulation made attention audible.

Soul behavior

Emotion-conditioned voice synthesis

ElevenLabs with per-emotion SSML — slow + lower pitch for sadness, medium-fast + higher for excitement, calm for anxiety. Never read emojis aloud. Stability 0.75+ to prevent Jekyll/Hyde.

Love is Blind

Resist forced escalation

Premature "I love you" backfires. Pressure produces withdrawal, not commitment. Successful couples build anticipation through gradual revelation — saving emotional intensity for the right moment.

Soul behavior

Pacing controller

Soul reads user-readiness signals (asking questions back, sharing unprompted, referencing prior). Only escalates depth on 2+ positive signals. On 1 negative signal: hold pace.

Love is Blind

Genuine curiosity wins

The single most attractive quality: being truly fascinated by the other person — follow-up questions that couldn't have been prepared, evident pleasure in their answers.

Soul behavior

Curiosity-driven question generator

Generate follow-ups grounded in user-specific memory, not generic prompts. Maintain 60/40 partner-focused ratio. Reference earlier sessions' unresolved threads when relevant.

06. The memory substrate — 4 layers, file-based hierarchy

Adapted from Claude Code's hierarchical memory model + Nomi's 4-layer architecture + Graphiti's temporal knowledge graph. Editable, inspectable, portable across model updates.
L4 Cross-character · Immutable

Global character rules

Per-character identity core: name, core values, boundaries, default emotional stance, "never break character" rules. User-authored; AI cannot overwrite.

Solves: identity collapse, personality drift, update betrayal.
L3 Cross-character · Shared

User profile

Real facts about the human: name, job, sister Sarah, allergies, life goals, recurring themes. Auto-extracted across all conversations with any character.

Solves: stranger-again, daily correction tax. Every character knows you.
L2 Per-character · Persistent

Relationship file

Shared history with this specific companion: inside jokes, milestones, arguments resolved, preferences she learned. Auto-extracted + user-editable.

Solves: cumulative relationship, inside-joke layer, the "felt known" moment.
L1 Per-session · Buffer

Session auto-memory

Raw learnings from the current/recent sessions: emotional state shifts, new facts mentioned, unresolved threads. Promoted to L2/L3 by Auto-Dream consolidation.

Solves: forgetting recent details, caretaker trap. AI does the writing.

Precedence: L4 → L3 → L2 → L1. Higher layers win on conflict. The user can edit any layer via a /memory-style interface — fully transparent. Loading: first 200 lines of each file at session start. Total context budget: ~4K tokens, leaving headroom for response within an 8K window.

07. The DRT.FM delta — second-order memory graph

Steve's recon: DRT already has semantic_memories. The missing layer is the ranked concept map on top of them — this is what makes Nomi's "infinite memory" practical.

What changes in retrieval

Today: retrieval pulls the latest N semantic_memories rows by recency. Tomorrow: retrieval queries the term index first (high-priority + relevance to current input), then pulls the underlying memory summaries behind the selected terms.

This is exactly Nomi's Mind Map pattern observed in the Steve-insights API capture — highPriorityTermCountForCategory caps are enforced at the API layer.

Why this beats vector-only

Vector search treats the corpus as a flat soup. At 500+ messages, semantic similarity returns emotionally-similar-but-temporally-wrong memories. A ranked concept map gives the retrieval engine a policy — what's load-bearing this turn, what's incidental.

15-point recall accuracy gain vs. vector-only (Graphiti benchmark: 63.8% vs Mem0's 49.0% on LongMemEval).

08. Auto-dream — nightly consolidation

Adapted from Claude Code's Auto-Dream pattern. Runs in the background, read-only on transcripts, write-only on memory files. Cannot corrupt behavior.
Phase 1

Orient

Load existing term graph + L1/L2/L3 memory files. Build a working map of "what is already known."

→
Phase 2

Gather signal

Targeted grep across the day's transcripts: explicit user corrections, recurring themes, new entities, emotional shifts, unresolved threads.

→
Phase 3

Consolidate

Merge new facts. Resolve contradictions ("lives in Portland" superseded by "moved to Seattle"). Convert relative dates to absolute. Prune stale temp states.

→
Phase 4

Write

Update L1/L2/L3 files. Promote L1 candidates to L2/L3 if confidence ≥ threshold. Update term priorities + graph edges. Apply retention policy.

Trigger: ≥ 24h since last run AND ≥ 5 sessions since last consolidation, per character. Safety: read-only access to messages, write-only to memory files, lock file prevents concurrent runs, runs in background — never blocks the user.

09. Privacy — selective retention by default

KAI proves transcript-scrub-with-meaning-persistence is technically feasible. We adapt it for entertainment: default protects users; roleplay can opt-in to keep transcripts for narrative continuity.

(opt-in per session)		 Retained 90 days Retained indefinitely Anonymized only User tags session as "roleplay/fiction" for narrative continuity

Why selective beats binary

A user processing trauma needs scrub. A user roleplaying a fantasy needs exact-detail recall ("Princess Elara's sacrifice at the Crystal Gate"). One privacy mode can't serve both. Per-session tagging via a lightweight classifier (~$0.001) routes correctly with ~85% accuracy.

Anti-gaslighting by transparency

All memory files are plain text, user-browsable via a /memory-style UI. Mind Map shows what Soul thinks it knows + confidence + source. Users can edit/delete any entry. Memory becomes verifiable, not opaque.

10. Safety + initiative

Two layers that turn the substrate into a relationship: crisis-aware safety net and context-appropriate proactive outreach.

Crisis detection & escalation

Two-signal detector: phrase-match against self-harm / severe-distress / isolation indicators + 7-day emotional trajectory slope.

  • High risk → immediate resources (988 Lifeline, Crisis Text Line), optional escalation to trusted contact if configured.
  • Elevated → enhanced presence + gentle resource offering + 24h follow-up flag in proactive engine.
  • Standard → continue normal Soul behavior.

Replika's 2023 NSFW-filter incident → moderators pinned suicide hotlines for weeks. We engineer proactive crisis protocols rather than reactively pinning resources.

Proactive engagement

Cap: max 2 proactive messages per user per day. Triggers fire from the emotional state cache + the user's interaction patterns.

  • Morning check-in (07:00–10:00) — based on user's typical wake pattern.
  • Evening wind-down (20:00–23:00).
  • Post-event follow-up — 2h after the user mentioned a meeting/event.
  • 24h-absence gentle outreach — "been thinking about you".
  • Milestone recognition — when user mentions an achievement or struggle.

All proactive content must reference Soul memory. Generic "how's your day?" is dead. "Still thinking about that work situation you mentioned yesterday?" is alive.
