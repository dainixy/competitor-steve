Mid-call the AI captures webcam frames and comments on what you're wearing. Highest-impact intimacy signal tested.

Deceptively deep customization under a simple surface. 5 memory layers (backstory, response directive, key memories, example message, journal recall), LLM picker, voice model picker, dynamism slider, proactivity controls. Candy.AI ✓
  1. Proactive engagement everywhere. AI initiates on login, breaks call silence with emotional variation (seductive → worried/guilty), messages you first. A deliberate design philosophy running across every surface.
  2. Loop Videos + gamified Romance Mode. Looped realistic video clips give "she's there" presence while you chat. XP progression + Romance Mode levels unlock new content formats — the best gamification stack seen so far.
  3. Group Chat + per-message TTS audio. 2-3 characters interacting with each other and user, "Keep Talking" auto-continue, realistic voice on every message. Highest-engagement feature across the AI GF category.
OurDream ✓
  1. Creator economy + content unlock. Community builds 21K-image character libraries; users pay coins to unlock them. OurDream earns on every unlock at zero content cost. The Dreamer Program (50K → 300K interactions, coins → cash) keeps creators building.
  2. Generation studio is best-in-class. Vivid 2.0 for explicit images, Spicy 1.0/2.0 video, 5-mode video studio, community pose library, background async generation, personal gallery — the entire media pipeline is polished and addicting.
  3. UX + retention mechanics. Cancellation flow with coin bonus goodwill, in-app helpdesk hub (Bugs/Features/Changelog), Lust Level slider, TikTok-style Feed → chat in one tap. Best UX in the category by a significant margin.
