Top 3 Standout Features Completed sessions

SecretDesires →
  1. Voice quality & pacing. Fastest, most natural speech cadence of any competitor — sounded genuinely human. Others speak too slowly and kill immersion. Sets the bar DRT.FM must match.
  2. Image generation speed & optimisation. DaVinci engine at 10–15 seconds, output at 35KB/832×1248 — fast and lean. Visual outfit picker with thumbnail examples makes generation intuitive.
  3. Partner settings depth + cancel flow. Lots of per-chat customisation options. Cancel flow has 2–3 escalating heart bribes (2× → 2.5×) plus a downgrade offer — the most thorough retention flow seen across all competitors.
Candy.AI →
  1. Proactive engagement everywhere. AI initiates on login, breaks call silence with emotional variation (seductive → worried/guilty), messages you first. A deliberate design philosophy running across every surface.
  2. Loop Videos + gamified Romance Mode. Looped realistic video clips give "she's there" presence while you chat. XP progression + Romance Mode levels unlock new content formats — the best gamification stack seen so far.
  3. Group Chat + per-message TTS audio. 2-3 characters interacting with each other and user, "Keep Talking" auto-continue, realistic voice on every message. Highest-engagement feature across the AI GF category.
OurDream →
  1. Creator economy + content unlock. Community builds 21K-image character libraries; users pay coins to unlock them. OurDream earns on every unlock at zero content cost. The Dreamer Program (50K → 300K interactions, coins → cash) keeps creators building.
  2. Generation studio is best-in-class. Vivid 2.0 for explicit images, Spicy 1.0/2.0 video, 5-mode video studio, community pose library, background async generation, personal gallery — the entire media pipeline is polished and addicting.
  3. UX + retention mechanics. Cancellation flow with coin bonus goodwill, in-app helpdesk hub (Bugs/Features/Changelog), Lust Level slider, TikTok-style Feed → chat in one tap. Best UX in the category by a significant margin.
GirlfriendGPT →
  1. UX/UI surface + Top Creators leaderboard. Best first visual impression of any competitor — sexy, shiny, modern. UGC creator economy (359 characters, 57M+ engagement scores) keeps content fresh. The look sells even when the product doesn't deliver.
  2. Video functionality — nobody else has it. Badly executed (generic anime renderer, no character awareness), but it sells subscriptions. First-mover signal: study Promptchan for the right implementation, but copy GGPT's decision to ship video at all.
  3. Notification system (Beamer) + cancellation/reactivation flow. 4-channel re-engagement loop for ~$50/mo. Exit survey captures churn reason; one-click reactivation keeps lapsed users recoverable. Both are rare in the category.
Kindroid →
  1. Talking-head live calls for all characters + camera vision. Self-hosted face-animation pipeline (V3) drives lips + face from any 2:3 photo — scales to the entire character library instantly. Mid-call the AI captures webcam frames and comments on what you're wearing. Highest-impact intimacy signal tested.
  2. Deceptively deep customization under a simple surface. 5 memory layers (backstory, response directive, key memories, example message, journal recall), LLM picker, voice model picker, dynamism slider, proactivity controls. Hidden in settings — most users never find them. Same depth with better discoverability = big opportunity.
  3. Community ecosystem with identity culture. Users call their characters "Kins" — possessive, attached. Social profiles, remix marketplace, 34K Reddit, 30K Discord with daily prompts and events, YouTube affiliate livestreams. Third-party community behaves like a fan community, not a product community.

How this works Process

  1. Dainis opens a paid account on a competitor site.
  2. Browses freely. Shares notes + screenshots in chat (screenshots auto-save to ~/Downloads/SCR-*.png|jpeg).
  3. Claude catalogues notes into the right page, moves screenshots into assets/checks/<slug>/, and prompts for Chrome DevTools deep-dives when a feature looks standout.
  4. At session end, Claude generates the Standout & Actions for Steve zone — verdict, steal-list, win-list, avoid-list, 1-10 scores.
  5. Then recommends the next competitor.

Always-On Checklist Every competitor

  1. Time it (seconds): first chat response · first image · first video · first voice word · clicks from homepage → first message.
  2. Screenshots to always grab: chat input area · subscription/upgrade page · homepage (logged in) · mobile view.
  3. Ick Test — flag any: AI breaking character · unexpected NSFW blocks · mid-chat upsell prompts · UI bugs · moments that felt genuinely "human."
  4. Score 1-10 at end: memory · voice emotional quality · image face consistency · NSFW consistency · overall "companion vs chatbot" feel.
  5. Steal: one thing to copy into DRT.FM immediately.
  6. Win: one thing DRT.FM already does better.
