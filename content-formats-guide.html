Creator Playbook 2025

20 Content Formats

Visual guide + 3 AI-powered ideas for every format

Camera-Based Editing-Heavy Data / Lists Reaction Teaching High Production
Unfiltered
Format 01
Yap
Pure high-energy talking to camera — raw, on the go, zero polish. The authenticity IS the format.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to generate a spicy hot-take on your niche — then yap your raw, unfiltered response to camera.
2Paste a Reddit thread into ChatGPT, get "the most outrageous take," then yap a live reaction while driving.
3AI writes 10 controversial opinions in your niche. You react to each one in 30 seconds. Film it all as one yap session, cut into clips.
Commentary
Format 02
Green Screen
You appear over a background (tweet, chart, article) to comment on it. The background does the heavy lifting visually.
3 AI Ideas
1Use Midjourney or Ideogram to generate a fake "leaked memo" or chart in your niche. Green-screen yourself reacting to it.
2Feed a news headline to AI, get a bold counter-argument, then present it green-screened over the original article screenshot.
3AI generates a "stats infographic" (use Canva AI or ChatGPT + code interpreter). You appear over it explaining what most people miss.
Structured
Format 03
Talking Head
Classic structured video essay — clean framing, good lighting, professional mic. You own the frame and deliver.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to write a full video script in your style (paste 3 past videos as examples). Read it cold — the teleprompter app PromptSmart works great.
2Ask AI to "steelman the opposite of my opinion on [topic]." Film yourself dismantling it argument by argument.
3Use Descript or Captions.ai to auto-subtitle the talking head and auto-punch-in on key phrases for dynamic cuts.
Multi-camera
Format 04
Interview
Dynamic conversation between host and guest. Multi-camera captures reactions, expressions, and energy exchange.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to generate 20 spiky interview questions for your guest based on their Twitter/X profile and last 10 posts. Pick the 7 sharpest.
2After filming, drop the transcript into Claude and ask: "Pull the 3 most quotable moments." Use those as captions/clips.
3Use a tool like Riverside.fm (built-in AI) to auto-generate a short-form highlight clip from the long-form interview.
Handheld
Format 05
Man on the Street
Unscripted public interviews. Gauge opinions, test knowledge, or create comedy by contrasting public answers with expert takes.
3 AI Ideas
1Prompt AI: "Give me 5 deceptively simple questions about [niche] that most people get wrong." These make perfect street questions.
2Use AI to write a "myth vs. fact" sheet before filming. Use people's wrong answers as the hook, then deliver the fact in your VO.
3After filming, use Whisper AI to transcribe all answers. Feed to Claude: "Find the funniest contrast between answers." Use that as your edit structure.
No Face
Format 06
Text with B-Roll
Cinematic footage + bold text on screen + voiceover. Story told without showing your face. Highest viral-per-effort ratio.
3 AI Ideas
1Use ElevenLabs to generate a cinematic voiceover from an AI-written script. Pair with free Pexels/Pixabay B-roll. Full video, zero camera time.
2Use Sora or Runway to generate custom B-roll that matches your script perfectly — no more generic stock footage.
3Ask AI to write a "cold open hook" (first 3 seconds = curiosity gap). Then build the text-B-roll video around revealing the answer.
Dual Attention
Format 07
Split Screen
Two content streams simultaneously — a visual hook (gameplay, satisfying video) alongside educational content to retain attention.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to write a "facts that feel illegal to know" script. Record yourself talking head. Pair with satisfying Subway Surfers gameplay below.
2AI generates a "rapid-fire quiz" in your niche. You read it aloud (top half), score ticker shown in the bottom half. Viewers track their own score.
3Use Runway or Pika to generate looping abstract/satisfying AI video for the bottom panel. Zero B-roll hunting needed.
Locked Tripod
Format 08
Clone
You interact with a duplicate of yourself — debate, skit, or two perspectives. Filmed twice on locked tripod, composited in edit.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to write a full debate script — "Past You vs. Current You" on a belief you've changed. Play both sides.
2Prompt: "Write a 60-second argument between a beginner and an expert about [topic]." Film both characters as yourself.
3Use HeyGen to create an AI avatar "clone" — one real you, one AI you. Run a dialogue between them with no filming needed at all.
3-Person Scene
Format 09
Triple Clone
Three versions of you simultaneously — Rookie, Manager, Expert — each representing a different perspective or knowledge tier.
3 AI Ideas
1Prompt AI: "Write a 90-second roundtable where Year 1 Me, Year 3 Me, and Year 10 Me debate [decision]." Film all three versions of yourself.
2AI writes three simultaneous commentary tracks (rookie, mid-level, expert) for the same situation — like a DVD director's commentary layered three ways.
3Use three different AI voices (ElevenLabs) to narrate three clones of yourself — no filming needed, just three avatars from HeyGen or D-ID.
PiP Reaction
Format 10
Video Reaction
Watch a video and give real-time commentary. Your facial expressions add emotion and commentary layer on top of the source content.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to identify the 5 most debated or controversial takes in your niche. React to those videos — pre-loaded controversy = algorithm fuel.
2After filming, use AI to transcribe your reaction and generate a "key timestamps" list for the description. Boosts SEO and watch time.
3Feed source video transcript to AI: "What would a skeptic say at each key claim?" Use those as your reaction talking points so you're never scrambling.
Tier Visual
Format 11
Ranking / Tier List
Gamified content ranking items S through F. The visual update of the tier list in real-time keeps viewers hooked to see where things land.
3 AI Ideas
1Prompt AI: "Give me 10 items in [niche] that people rank differently. Include one wildly controversial pick." Use that as your tier-list lineup.
2After publishing, feed top comments back to AI: "What items did viewers disagree with most?" Film a "revisited" tier list responding to the controversy.
3Use Canva AI or TierMaker to auto-generate the tier list graphic. Feed in your items, export, overlay in CapCut with drag animations.
Fast Cuts
Format 12
Listicle
Bullet-point content delivered rapidly. Each item is a micro-dopamine hit. Numbered overlays tell viewers exactly where they are.
3 AI Ideas
1Ask AI: "Give me 7 [niche] tips — each must be surprising and under 15 words." Film one talking-head clip per item. Cut fast.
2Use Claude to write the hooks for each list item first (the punchline), then build backward from those. Strongest list items = best retention.
3Generate the on-screen text cards with AI + Canva. Batch create all 7 cards in one Canva session. Film once, overlay the cards in post.
Physical Prop
Format 13
Object Lesson
A physical object demonstrates an abstract concept. The tactile anchor makes complex ideas instantly memorable and shareable.
3 AI Ideas
1Prompt: "Give me 10 everyday objects that can teach a lesson about [niche concept]." AI often surfaces non-obvious metaphors that feel original.
2Use AI to script the reveal moment — the point where the object's metaphor clicks. That's the viral clip. Film just that 15-second moment separately.
3Use AI image generators to mock up "explainer overlays" of the object (arrows, labels) to add in post — makes the lesson even cleaner.
Debunk Format
Format 14
Play / Pause Reaction
You actively pause a video mid-claim to debunk, elaborate, or fact-check before letting it continue. Creates interrupt-driven tension.
3 AI Ideas
1Find a popular video in your niche. Feed its transcript to AI: "Identify every factually wrong or misleading claim." Those become your pause points.
2Use AI to pre-write your "pause commentary" for each timestamp before filming. You'll be sharper and won't ramble on camera.
3Ask AI: "What is the most commonly repeated bad advice in [niche]?" Find a video that says it. Play/pause your way through debunking it.
Birds-Eye
Format 15
Top Down
Overhead camera pointing straight down at a desk. Perfect for sketching, journaling, food, handwriting, or product unboxing.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to design a simple "visual framework" (boxes, arrows, labels). Print it out, then draw over it by hand on camera for an organic, visual breakdown.
2AI generates a "daily planning template" for your niche audience. Film yourself filling it in from overhead — relatable morning routine content.
3Use Procreate + iPad overhead as your "canvas." Ask AI to outline a mind map structure, then illustrate it live from above in real-time.
Slide Deck
Format 16
Carousel
Walk through a swipeable slide deck on video. Native to Instagram/LinkedIn carousels translated into a video walkthrough format.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to write a 7-slide carousel script. Feed each slide to Canva's AI presentation tool to auto-generate the visual design.
2Ask Claude: "Turn this blog post into a 6-slide carousel. Each slide needs a hook line and 2 bullet points." Record a voiceover walkthrough.
3Use Gamma.app (AI presentation builder) to generate the entire carousel in 60 seconds. Record your screen walking through it.
Draw Live
Format 17
Whiteboard
Real-time drawing on a physical or digital board. Watching something get built visually creates stronger comprehension than slides alone.
3 AI Ideas
1Ask AI: "Design a simple visual framework for [concept] using only circles, arrows, and boxes." Then draw it live on camera. AI = your art director.
2Use Excalidraw (free, hand-drawn look) + AI prompts to pre-build the diagram digitally. Record a screen-share walkthrough instead of physical board.
3Use AI to write a "whiteboard explainer script" timed to each drawing beat. Pre-practice with the script so the draw-and-talk feels effortless.
Community-Driven
Format 18
Ask Me Anything
Answer direct questions from your audience. The pinned comment on screen creates accountability and shows the community you listen.
3 AI Ideas
1Feed your comment section to AI: "Rank these questions by how interesting/viral the answer could be." Film your answers in that order.
2No comments yet? Ask AI to simulate "the 10 questions your ideal audience most wants answered." Film an AMA before you have one.
3Use AI to prep a crisp 45-second answer structure for each question: hook → answer → example → twist. Tight answers = better completion rate.
High Production
Format 19
Hyper-Cinematic
Mood over information. Shallow depth of field, deliberate camera moves, color grading, pro audio. The aesthetic IS the message.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to write a deeply poetic, short monologue (40-60 words) for your niche. Film it cinematic-style with dramatic pauses and lens flares.
2Use Sora/Runway to generate ultra-cinematic establishing shots that would cost thousands to film. Pair with your own voiceover for zero-budget high production.
3Feed your script to AI: "Rewrite this in a cinematic, Christopher Nolan narration style." The writing upgrade alone changes the feel of your footage.
Frame Shift
Format 20
Change of Aspect Ratio
The frame literally changes mid-video — cinematic letterbox bars drop in for emphasis, then snap back. The format itself creates drama.
3 AI Ideas
1Use AI to identify the "mic drop moment" in your script — the most impactful single sentence. That's when you trigger the letterbox shift.
2Ask AI: "Write 5 one-sentence statements about [niche] that feel like movie quotes." Film them. Add letterbox bars and a cinematic score clip for each.
3Use CapCut's "cinematic crop" keyframe animation to automate the aspect ratio shift in post. No complex editing needed — two keyframes is all it takes.
