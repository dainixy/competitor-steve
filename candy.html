Standout & Actions for Steve Session complete · 2026-05-20

Candy AI is the most complete product we've reviewed. It doesn't feel like a chatbot with features bolted on — it feels like a platform designed around emotional retention. Every layer is deliberate: proactive messages pull you in, XP/Romance Mode progression keeps you advancing, Loop Videos give presence without live generation, and the token economy is engineered so you're always just short of the next thing you want. Dainis rates it alongside OurDream as the top two competitors to learn from. Voice/audio experience is best-in-class. UI is polished and clean throughout.

Steal immediately for DRT.FM

  • Proactive engagement everywhere. AI initiates on login, breaks silence on call with emotional variation (seductive → worried/guilty), messages you first. Apply this pattern across DRT: login greeting, call silence-breaker, chat re-engagement after inactivity.
  • Loop Videos for presence. Looped 5-10s realistic video clips of the character (basic movements, smiles, poses) play while you chat — she's "there" without live generation. Solves the talking-head gap without Kindroid-style complexity. Build a small library of idle loops per character.
  • Group Chat + Keep Talking. 2-3 characters interacting with each other and the user, per-message TTS audio, auto-continue button. Highly engaging multi-character scenes. Users clearly love it across all AI GF sites.
  • "Change Face 🪙1" in character creator. Regenerate just the face while keeping all other settings. Removes the #1 frustration in AI character creation. Cheap for user, high satisfaction impact.
  • Gamification stack: XP + Romance Mode levels + content format variety. XP per good message, levels unlock new content types (calls, private content, Live Action cutscenes). Combines progression with genuine new feature unlocks — not just cosmetic rewards. Layer this over DRT's existing relationship system.

DRT.FM already does better

  • Talking head / live video call. Candy's Loop Video is a clever workaround but it's pre-recorded and disconnected from chat. DRT's ambition for a real-time responsive talking head (Kindroid-style or better) is a higher bar that Candy hasn't hit.
  • Memory system. Not tested on Candy — but DRT's focus on deep persistent memory is a differentiation Candy hasn't foregrounded.

Avoid / counter-position

  • Token economy designed to frustrate. Premium Plus includes 230 tokens/month but cheapest private content unlock is 260t — mathematically impossible to access without buying more. Users notice this (Trustpilot complaints). DRT should give enough included tokens to feel genuinely generous at each tier.
  • Private content as pre-generated static library. Unlocks are "Hot Tub Fucking" style pre-made clips, some feeling dated. Counter-position: DRT's NSFW content should be dynamic/personalized, not a static vault you're unlocking.
  • No prompt control on image/video gen. You can't direct what's in the scene — model decides. Power users frustrated. DRT should give at minimum basic scene direction.
  • Flash Boobs as a blunt token drain. Named NSFW video style that bypasses chat escalation entirely. Effective for revenue but feels transactional. DRT's approach of earning escalation through relationship feels healthier long-term.

2026-05-20 · standout · product-insight · presence-without-voice
Dainis observation: looping video + text chat = a third path to "presence" that nobody else is doing.

Kindroid and DRT's Alexis solve the "real person feel" problem with a talking head — live lipsync video + voice. Candy solves it differently: high-quality realistic video loops (she moves, shifts, looks around) running alongside text chat. No AI voice, no uncanny lipsync. She just exists in the frame while you talk to her in text.

The effect: feels like talking to a real attractive person who happens to be on camera. The video is pre-rendered so quality is high and consistent. The text chat is responsive. Together they create presence without real-time generation cost or voice awkwardness.

Why this works when talking heads often don't: AI lipsync is still slightly off, AI voices are still slightly flat. Candy sidesteps both problems entirely by decoupling video from speech. The video is beautiful because it's not trying to do anything in real-time. The chat is smart because it's just a language model. Neither is fighting the other.

For Steve / DRT: this is worth considering as an alternative or complement to voice. A looping high-quality video presence layer + text/voice chat could feel more premium than a real-time talking head at lower generation cost and higher visual quality. The ceiling on pre-rendered video quality is much higher than real-time.
