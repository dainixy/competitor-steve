Cross-Platform Synthesis — What This Means

Universal Pain Points (Every Platform Fails Here)

#1
Memory failures destroy immersion
The single most-mentioned complaint across ALL five platforms. Candy: "like Alzheimer's" (60% of all reviews). OurDream: resets between sessions on lower tiers. Joi: limits on free plan. Promptchan: context lost across sessions.

This is the biggest unmet need in the entire category. Any platform that solves persistent, reliable memory — and makes it visible to the user — owns a massive retention advantage.
#2
Token/coin systems cause rage-cancels
Every single platform layers a secondary currency on top of subscription. Every single platform generates resentment for it. Candy ($300/month extra), OurDream (50 coins/minute for voice calls), Joi ($20 per extra character), Promptchan (gems per message).

Users don't hate paying — they hate not knowing what they're paying for. "Constantly calculating actual expenses" is the exact quote. Flat pricing with clear limits wins trust.
#3
Voice is the most-wanted, least-delivered feature
Joi has zero voice. OurDream's is "flat and emotionless, doesn't adapt during intimate scenes." Candy's is rated 3.1/5 "robotic." Promptchan's lags and doesn't match context.

Users don't care about TTS — they want voice that reacts. That speeds up, gets breathy, laughs. This is the emotional core of the product and nobody has solved it.
#4
NSFW censorship on NSFW platforms
Candy, OurDream, Joi, Promptchan all have inconsistent content moderation that randomly blocks explicitly adult conversations on platforms marketed as uncensored.

"Wannabe NSFW, but failing." "Characters reject roleplay despite premium promises." This breaks trust immediately. Users subscribed specifically for this — getting blocked mid-scene is an immediate cancellation trigger.
