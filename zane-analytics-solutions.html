Zane Analytics — Three Solutions

Cross-platform analytics for 7 Blotato-scheduled channels, designed for AI diagnosis with minimum human involvement.

Mental model

You want to answer one question: why did some channels lose views? That question splits three ways:

  • Sudden cliff → account flag / algorithm hit. Needs traffic-source data to detect.
  • Slow decay → content fit drift. Needs engagement trend + retention curves.
  • Never took off → cold-start failure. Needs follower-vs-non-follower reach split.

Blotato gives you views + engagement only. That's enough to tell you that a channel is dying. It's not enough to tell you why — for that you need retention curves (YouTube) and traffic source (Meta/TikTok). The three solutions below trade off how aggressively you go after that "why" signal.

Why this is the recommendation

  • The single highest-value signal across all 7 channels is the YouTube retention curve, because YouTube is your highest-cadence channel (3×/day) and you can directly see whether viewers drop at second 1 (hook fail), second 15 (body fail), or second 45 (no payoff). This signal is free and takes 30 minutes to wire up.
  • Apify gives you 80% of the IG/TT depth without Meta/TikTok's business-account approval gauntlet. If a scraper breaks, you replace it; you don't wait 2 weeks for app re-approval.
  • Facebook/Threads/Twitter are likely cold-start losers regardless — pouring engineering effort into deep analytics there before fixing the content-fit problem is misallocated. Solution C escalates only if needed.

Failure modes

  • Apify selector breakage when TikTok/IG update their HTML. Mitigation: weekly "got data?" health check; swap actors if needed (multiple competing actors exist).
  • YouTube Analytics API has a 1–3 day data freshness lag. Pull data 48h after post for stable numbers.
  • No retention data on the 6 non-YT channels. If the dead channels turn out to be TT/IG specifically, we still won't know why the body failed there — only that it did.

Recommendation

Start with B this week. Reconsider C in 30 days.

The reason: you said you have no clue which channels died. Before investing 2–4 weeks in app reviews, spend 2 days on Solution B so you actually know the shape of the problem. The YouTube retention curve alone — free, 30 minutes — will probably tell you 70% of what you need.

Decision point at day 30: if the dead channels are Meta/TikTok and you need follower-vs-non-follower reach split or demographics to diagnose, escalate to Metricool ($58/mo, no build work, ready in 2 days) rather than the full app-review path. Skip C1 unless you have a specific reason to want raw API access.

Rejected options

  • X API Basic ($200/mo) — not worth it for a low-priority channel at this volume. Use Blotato data; revisit only if X unexpectedly becomes a top-3 channel.
  • Phyllo (pay-per-call developer API) — no clear advantage over native APIs + Apify mix at this scale; gets expensive fast.
  • SocialInsider / Sprout / HeyOrca — agency-priced ($100–300/mo). Overkill for one operator.
  • Hand-screenshotting native dashboards — violates the "minimum human involvement" requirement.
  • Building a custom scraping stack with Playwright self-hosted — Apify already solves this; don't reinvent.
